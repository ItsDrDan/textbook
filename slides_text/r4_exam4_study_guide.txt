Chemistry 3B – Exam 4 – Chapters 21-24 
Key Skills 
This document provides a list of key skills from each chapter that students are expected to be able to perform for the exam. Skills may be tested individually, combined with other skills, or not at all. Although the exam will primarily focus on chapters since the previous exam, students are expected to know concepts from earlier in the semester such as identifying functional groups, and common reactions of each functional group (oxidation, reduction, addition, hydrolysis, etc) 
 Overall 
I don’t expect you to memorize the order of reactions, or names of compounds, of any process! 
Know what acronyms stand for (ATP, ADP, AMP, FAD, NAD+, CoA, etc) and parts that make up the overall molecule 
Know general order of events for metabolism of carbohydrates, lipids and proteins 
Know where in the body/cell various processes occur – that’s the extent of biology covered. 
Be able to identify types of reactions based on reactions & products:
Oxidation, reduction, phosphorylation, phosphate transfer, carboxylation, decarboxylation, hydrogenation, dehydrogenation, hydration, dehydration etc. 
Be able to deduce product based on type of reaction.

Chapter 22 
Define catabolic reactions and anabolic reactions.
Catabolic reactions: complex molecules broken down. Usually releases energy (exothermic)
Anabolic reactions: small molecules are joined together to build larger ones. Usually requires energy (endothermic)
Know the three stages of catabolism: 
Stage 1: Digestion
Stage 2: Degradation and some oxidation 
Stage 3: Oxidation

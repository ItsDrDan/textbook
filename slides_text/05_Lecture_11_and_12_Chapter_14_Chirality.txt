--- slide 10 ---
Stereoisomers: Chirality
Stereoisomers always have the same connectivity of atoms, but different arrangement in space
A stereocenter is a carbon that is bonded to four different groups
Chiral objects have non-superimposable mirror images

--- slide 11 ---
Achirality
A molecule that is superimposable on its mirror image is achiral.
Two socks from a pair are mirror images that are superimposable

--- slide 18 ---
Biological Connections
Many drugs are chiral, and often they must interact with a chiral receptor to be effective.
One enantiomer of a drug may be effective in treating a disease whereas its mirror image may be ineffective.

--- slide 19 ---
Chirality Matters!
Two different enantiomers can interact differently with smell receptors in the brain and have different perceived odors.

--- slide 20 ---
Chirality Matters!
Naproxen (the pain reliever in Aleve) has a toxic enantiomer, so care must be taken in its  manufacture.

Fischer Projections
A Fischer projection takes a 3D tetrahedral shape, and re-draws it:
places the most oxidized group at the top.
uses vertical lines for bonds that go back.
uses horizontal lines for bonds that go forward.

Conclusions
Alcohols can add to the carbonyl group making hemiacetals, hemiketals, acetals and ketals
Stereoisomers have the same connectivity of atoms, but in different orientations in space.
A chiral molecule must have at least one chiral center (stereocenter)

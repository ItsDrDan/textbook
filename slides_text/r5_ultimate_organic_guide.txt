ACID/BASE Reactions
Acids donate protons 
Bases receive protons
Carboxylic acids are organic acids
Amines are organic bases
Water is amphoteric meaning it can be either an acid or a base depending on what it reacts with

Chapter 12: Alkanes
Saturated vs unsaturated hydrocarbons
How to draw skeletal to condensed structures and vice versa
Isomers = same formula, but different structures
Structural/Constitutional Isomers
Same formula, but different skeleton
Cis/trans isomers
Feel comfortable with drawing isomers to determine total number possible for a simple formula
Approximately 5-9 Isomer questions… do not sleep on this…
Know how to predict Addition Reactions
Alkenes are the reactants and we “Open the double doors”
Hydrogen wants to make the most friends
Know reactants and products
Hydrogenation (H2)
Hydrohalogenation (HCl, HBr, HI, HF)
Hydration (H2O)
Halogenation (Cl2, Br2, F2, I2)
Know how to recognize elimination reactions 
Predator vs Prey Mentality – Remove lonely hydrogen and functional groups
Remove the following:
dehydrogenation (H2)
dehydrohalogenation (HCl, HBr, HI, HF)
dehydration (H2O)
dehalogenation (Cl2, Br2, F2, I2)
Know the shapes of alkyl branches
3 carbons are propyl and iso propyl (Y-shape_
4 carbons is butyl, isobutyl (Yshape), sec-butyl, and t-butyl
Know ortho,meta, and para
Ortho = next door neighbors (1,2)
Meta = nothing in the middle (1,3)
Para is opposite (1,4)
Know how to recognize Cis/trans
Know that cis/trans with same formula are isomers of one another
Chapter 13: Alcohols, Ethers, Thiols
Know how to classify alcohols
Go to carbon alcohol is connected to… then count the C-C bonds
Primary is  R-CH2-OH
Secondary is R-CH (R) – OH
Tertiary is R-CR (R) – OH 
Know Oxidation Reactions 
Primary Alcohol to Aldehyde to Carboxylic Acid
Secondary Alcohol to Ketone
Tertiary = NR
Know how to identify a thiol (-SH)
Reaction we did not do, but similar to others.
Thiol + carboxylic acid = esterthiol via condensation
Chapter 14: Ketones and Aldehydes
Know Reduction Reactions (backwards of oxidation)
Carboxylic Acids goes to Aldehyde then to primary alcohol
Ketone turns into secondary alcohol
Know how to recognize hemiacetals, acetals
Originally an aldehyde and alcohol 
Find anomeric carbon (google eye O’s) classification is primary
Know how to recognize hemiketals, ketals
Originally a ketone and alcohol 
Find anomeric carbon (google eye O’s) classification is secondary
Chapter 15: Chirality
Know how to count chiral centers.
Know enantiomers and diastereomers using wedges and dashes
Don’t forget you can rotate and flip molecules
Diastereomers are when you have two chiral centers that have one differing wedge and dash (LOOK AT PICTURE BELOW)
RACEMIC MIXTURE = term we never talked about 
Is an equal ratio of a pair of enantiomers
Chapter 16: Carboxylic Acids and Esters
Ester Reactions are acid and basic hydrolysis
Acid hydrolysis (Ester cut in to carboxylic acid and alcohol)	
Base Hydrolysis (Ester cut into Carboxylate salt and alcohol)
How to Create Esters (Condensation Reaction)
Alcohol + Carboxylic Acid = Ester
Double bond O side of the molecule is the original carboxylic acid
Chapter 18: Amines and Amides
How to Classify Amines (primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary) 
Counting # of carbon bonds connected to nitrogen. Quaternary is ammonium, 4 bonds
How to Create Amides (Condensation Reaction)
Amine + Carboxylic Acid = Amide
Double bond O side of the molecule is the original carboxylic acid

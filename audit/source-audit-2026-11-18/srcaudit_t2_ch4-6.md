**Top P1 items**
1. **597103, fn 5:** the sentence about the main gas-phase health-risk contributors in wildfire smoke is cited to Navarro et al. 2021, which does not make that claim. It only used acrolein, benzene and formaldehyde to estimate firefighter exposure. The claim comes from O'Dell et al. 2020 (ES&T), which also names hydrogen cyanide.
2. **597093, To Your Health:** the methanol doses ("as little as 10 mL… blindness… about 30 mL (1 oz) can be fatal") are attributed inline to the UKHSA overview. That page says 4–10 mL may cause permanent damage and gives a lethal dose of 300–1,000 mg/kg. It never states 30 mL or 1 oz.

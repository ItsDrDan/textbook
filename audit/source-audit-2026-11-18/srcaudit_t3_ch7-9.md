Top P1 items:
1. 597144: "The average daily diet should contain about 4–6 g of the essential fatty acids" is wrong. The DRI Adequate Intakes are about 12–17 g/day linoleic acid plus 1.1–1.6 g/day α-linolenic acid.
2. 597136: "normal urine may contain anywhere from a trace to 20 mg glucose/dL urine" is wrong. MedlinePlus gives 0–15 mg/dL (0–0.8 mmol/L).
3. 597139: "The incidence of galactosemia in the United States is 1 in every 65,000 newborn babies" is outside the current figure. MedlinePlus gives 1 in 30,000 to 60,000.
4. 597145 footnote 5 (Loften et al., J Dairy Sci 2014) is a BROKEN link: the uidaho.edu URL now redirects to a 404 page. The paper is real; replace the link with its DOI.

## 6. Recommendation: "Sources for data" note

**Recommendation:** put one short "Sources for Data" section in the front matter, after "Measurements and Uncertainty". Do not add per-lab notes. Few labs cite more than one or two outside values, and per-lab footnotes would clutter the Report Sheets. For the 2–3 history and health claims (Exp 10 aspirin history, Exp 14 RDA), add a short parenthetical source in the sentence, for example "(NIH Office of Dietary Supplements)". Draft, using only sources verified in this audit:

> **Sources for Data**
> Physical constants, hazard classifications, and reference values in this manual come from the following sources. Hazard information was current in November 2026; always check the Safety Data Sheet for the product you use.
> - **Melting points and molar masses:** PubChem, National Library of Medicine (https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov), compound records for each substance; *CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics*.
> - **Hazard classifications (GHS):** PubChem "Safety and Hazards" sections (ECHA C&L Inventory aggregated classifications) and supplier Safety Data Sheets. Carcinogen classification of methylene chloride: IARC Monographs, Vol. 110 (2017), Group 2A; regulation of laboratory use: U.S. EPA, 40 CFR Part 751, Subpart B (89 FR 39254, 2024).
> - **Electronegativity values (Appendix D) and atomic weights (Appendix F):** Pauling values as tabulated in PubChem's Periodic Table (https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/periodic-table/) and the *CRC Handbook*; IUPAC Commission on Isotopic Abundances and Atomic Weights, Standard Atomic Weights (https://www.ciaaw.org).
> - **Nomenclature (Appendices B and C):** Favre, H. A.; Powell, W. H. *Nomenclature of Organic Chemistry: IUPAC Recommendations and Preferred Names 2013*; Royal Society of Chemistry, 2014.
> - **Drug standards (Experiment #10):** United States Pharmacopeia, Aspirin Tablets and Buffered Aspirin Tablets monographs. History of aspirin: Desborough, M. J. R.; Keeling, D. M. *Br. J. Haematol.* **2017**, 177, 674–683.
> - **Enzyme data (Experiment #13):** Butterworth, P. J.; Warren, F. J.; Ellis, P. R. *Starch/Stärke* **2011**, 63, 395–405 (human α-amylase); Silverman, D. N. *Can. J. Bot.* **1991**, 69, 1070–1078 (carbonic anhydrase).
> - **Nutrition values (Experiment #14):** NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, Vitamin C and Vitamin B12 Fact Sheets (https://ods.od.nih.gov).
> - **Strawberry genome (Experiment #15):** Edger, P. P. et al. *Nat. Genet.* **2019**, 51, 541–547.

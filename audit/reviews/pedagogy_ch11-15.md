# Instructional-quality review, Chapters 11–15 (2026-11-13)

Read-only review; nothing in the repo was edited. Scores are 0–4 in this order: accuracy / clarity / prerequisite access / visuals / reasoning transparency / practice alignment / transfer. Visual scores come from alt text, captions and placement only; no image was opened. Chapters 11–12 were read by the lead reviewer; Chapters 13, 14 and 15 were each read by a separate reader working from the same brief, and their BEFORE strings were re-verified here.

## Patterns across the five chapters

- **Double telling is the main weakness.** On most adapted pages the inherited text and the Lecture Connection teach the same content in two vocabularies (11.2, 11.3, 12.1, 12.3–12.6, 13.1, 13.4, 14.2, 14.4, 15.3–15.5, 15.7). Worst cases: 14.2 (cycle and chain each told twice, then again in 14.3/14.4), 14.4 (chain three times, cyanide four times), 15.4 ("cycles" vs "repetitions"; "stage 3" means two different things), 15.5 (DKA story five times).
- **The route pages promise Try It questions "in each section", but about half the sections have none**, and several have no practice of any kind (11.1, 11.3, 11.5, 11.6, 12.1, 12.4, 12.7, 13.2, 15.6, 15.11). Some widgets sit on a different page from the content they test (activity plots on 11.4, not 11.3; digestion on 15.3, not 15.1; transamination on 15.10, not 15.8).
- **Inherited worked examples state answers; house examples reason and check.** The Problem → Solution → Check examples written for the course are consistently good. The inherited ones (11.2 Example 1, 12.3 Example 2, 12.6 Example 1) give an answer or a list of rules with no check.
- **Content taught before its section.** Genetic code in 12.4 before 12.6; glycolysis ATP bookkeeping by step number in 13.4 before 13.5; P site/A site tested in 12.5 before 12.6; ketone bodies in a 15.4 Try It before 15.5.
- **Valley and clinical boxes vary in whether they need chemistry.** Strong: 11.4 organophosphates, 12.9 Valley fever PCR, 13.9 glucose meter, 14.4 carbon monoxide, 15.5 ketones. Answerable without chemistry or with no question at all: 11.5 folic acid tortilla, 12.7 heel stick, 15.7 beans and tortillas.
- **Symbols and terms with no bridge.** ΔG (11.1, 13.1) and ΔG°′ (13.2); "coordinate covalent bonds" (11.5); "first committed step" (11.6); fructose 2,6-bisphosphate (13.8); reduction potential and volts (14.4); malonyl CoA as a signal in 15.3 before 15.6.
- **Practice Problems pages** all follow the same sound ramp from recall to board-style cases. Answers almost never name the section they come from, although every route page says to "reread the section it comes from". In the 14.7 and 15.13 board-style case sets, every correct choice is A.
- **ATP arithmetic:** every tally met in Chapters 13–15 was recomputed against the 2.5 / 1.5 / 10 / 30–32 / 106 convention and agrees. No contradictions between pages in any ATP number.

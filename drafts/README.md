The live LibreTexts pages have been edited since these drafts were last updated. As of 2026-11-11, every Guide and Problems page has text that is on LibreTexts but not here, and a few sentences here that LibreTexts has since changed.

**Do not rebuild Chapter Guides or Practice Problems from these drafts and sync them**, or the live edits will be lost. LibreTexts is the source of truth; `pages/live/` holds the 2026-11-11 export of every page.

The 2026-11-11 changes (Daniel Kwasniewski in credit lines, ten essential amino acids, corrected opener captions) were applied here as well.

## 1. Fresno County almond share (597145, 9.2 Valley Connection)
- **The old claim was confirmed.** Almond Almanac 2023, printed p. 32 (PDF p. 34), "California Almond Production by County" (million lb): Fresno 518.3 of 2,571.3 = **20.2%** for 2022/23. The receipts table on the same page also says "20.2%".
- **The newer Almanac is used.** Almond Almanac 2025 (Annual Report 2025), printed p. 24 (PDF p. 26): Fresno 559.5 of 2,713.5 million lb = **20.6%** for 2024/25, the largest county.
- **World share and farms:** p. 29 (PDF p. 31) gives the U.S. at 73% of world production in 2024/25. It also says "more than 7,600 almond farmers in California: NEARLY 90% ARE FAMILY FARMS".
- **Old "nearly 80%" wording:** that was the 2023 Almanac's own phrase (p. 4), but its pie chart showed the U.S. at 76%. The new text uses the chart value.
- **Cross-check (USDA NASS):** the 2025 California Almond Objective Measurement Report, p. 1, gives "last year's crop of 2.73 billion meat pounds". That agrees with the Almanac total of 2,713.5 million lb (handler receipts).
- **Edit:** the first sentence of the box now reads "In the 2024/25 crop year the United States, almost all of it California, grew about 73% of the world's almonds. California has more than 7,600 almond farms, nearly 90% of them family farms, and Fresno County alone produced about 21% of the state's crop." Source 1 now points to the Almanac 2025, pp. 24 and 29.

2. **Clinical accuracy and tone** – medical terms used correctly and defined on first use; no treatment advice, no dosing; hedged where evidence is soft; nursing/dental content ties to the chemistry taught. Remove anything speculative.
3. **Scope** – every topic sits inside the COR unit(s) for that chapter; flag and remove anything beyond (mechanisms, S/R assignment, Newman projections, detailed pharmacology). Confirm every COR bullet for the chapter is actually taught somewhere (book section or guide) – if one is missing, add it concisely.

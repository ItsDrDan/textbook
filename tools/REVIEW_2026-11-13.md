# Fresh full check: CHEM 3B textbook (2026-11-13)

Report from the check. **Update, 2026-11-13: batch M (the ✔ rows) was published on LibreTexts on the instructor's OK; nothing is committed or pushed.** The fixes are in `tools/batchM_2026-11-13.json`, built by `tools/batchM_build.py`.

## How this was done
- **Live vs. repo.** All 199 pages were fetched in the signed-in Browser pane (`/@api/deki/pages/<id>/contents?mode=raw&format=html&dream.out.format=json`) and hashed with SHA-256. **All 199 match `pages/live/`. No drift.**
- **Reading.** Nine independent readers each read their pages in full (Chapters 1–15, front and back matter) against `CONVENTIONS.md` and `ISSUE_LEDGER.md`, and re-solved about 1,430 problems, examples and exercises. A tenth checked the bank and ADAPT export.
- **Scripts (mine).** Scope keywords on every page; link text against link target for all 1,065 `/@go/page` links; 1,227 "Section N.M" mentions; figure numbering (`figscan.py`); 544 distinct external links and video embeds; natural size of all 1,128 images; text sizes in the attached PDF (`Full.pdf`, 2026-11-13 00:42, 1,338 pp., which already includes batch L).
- **What "confidence" means.** High = I or a reader checked the raw HTML, the image or the arithmetic. Medium = the fact is standard but was not checked against a source today. Low = a judgement call or a from-memory value.
- **Limits.** LibreTexts-hosted images were judged from alt text and captions, except where noted "image checked". Statistics in Valley Connection boxes were not re-verified. Links that answer 403 to scripts (CDC, ChemistryViews and others, 30 in all) could not be confirmed either way.

**"M" column:** ✔ = a draft edit is in the batch M JSON. A blank means it needs your decision, a source check or a new drawing first.

## Bottom line
- **No wrong answer key anywhere.** Inline answers and print keys agree on every page that has a key.
- **No stale cross-references from the 2026-11-12 renumbering.** Every "Section 12.x / 13.x / 15.x" mention and every link matches the current titles, including the Index, Glossary, Alignment and Source Attributions pages.
- **ATP numbers are consistent everywhere** (2.5 / 1.5 / 10; 30–32; 106; 120; −2; 7.3 kcal; 35%).
- **Scope is clean.** No pKa, R/S, E/Z, Henderson–Hasselbalch, meso, Newman or chair-flip content. One curved arrow remains on a lecture slide (8.4), and "hydride" is used in several places (see Tier 2).
- **What is left is small:** 14 wrong facts (most are inherited source values), one convention that leaks in seven places (thiamine and biotin called "cofactors"), seven pages whose exercise answers never print, and print-size and credit-line gaps.

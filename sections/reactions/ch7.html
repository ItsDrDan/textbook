Reactions at a Glance: Amines and Amides

Every reaction in this chapter that you are expected to write. Learn each one as a pattern: what reacts, what is added, what changes.

ReactionWhat happensTaught in
Amine as a baseAn amine accepts H+ from an acid: an ammonium ion (amine salt).Section 7.3
AmidationCarboxylic acid + amine (or NH3) with heat: a condensation that makes an amide and H2O.Section 7.6
Acid hydrolysis of an amideWater and acid break the C–N bond: carboxylic acid + ammonium ion.Section 7.7
Base hydrolysis of an amideNaOH breaks the C–N bond: carboxylate salt + amine (or NH3).Section 7.7

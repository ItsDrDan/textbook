Reactions at a Glance: Alkenes

Every reaction in this chapter that you are expected to write. Learn each one as a pattern: what reacts, what is added, what changes.

ReactionWhat happensTaught in
HydrogenationH2 with a Pt or Ni catalyst adds across the C=C; the alkene becomes an alkane.Section 3.4
Halogenation (addition)X2 adds across the C=C, one halogen on each carbon (a dihaloalkane).Section 3.4
HydrohalogenationHX adds across the C=C: H on one carbon, X on the other (an alkyl halide).Section 3.4
HydrationH2O with an acid catalyst adds across the C=C as H and OH, making an alcohol.Section 3.4

Reactions at a Glance: Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Every reaction in this chapter that you are expected to write. Learn each one as a pattern: what reacts, what is added, what changes.

ReactionWhat happensTaught in
EsterificationCarboxylic acid + alcohol, acid catalyst and heat: a condensation that makes an ester and H2O.Section 6.7
Acid hydrolysis of an esterWater and acid split the ester back into a carboxylic acid and an alcohol.Section 6.8
SaponificationNaOH and heat split the ester into a carboxylate salt and an alcohol.Section 6.8
Neutralization of a carboxylic acidA base (NaOH) removes the acidic H: carboxylate ion + water.Section 6.4

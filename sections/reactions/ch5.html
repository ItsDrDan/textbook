Reactions at a Glance: Aldehydes and Ketones

Every reaction in this chapter that you are expected to write. Learn each one as a pattern: what reacts, what is added, what changes.

ReactionWhat happensTaught in
Oxidation of an aldehyde[O] (including Tollens’ and Benedict’s reagents): –CHO becomes –COOH. Ketones do not react.Section 5.4
ReductionH2 with Ni adds across the C=O: an aldehyde gives a 1° alcohol, a ketone gives a 2° alcohol.Section 5.4
Hemiacetal formationOne alcohol adds across the C=O; the carbon ends up with –OH and –OR.Section 5.3
Acetal formationTwo alcohols react with the C=O (acid catalyst); the carbon ends up with two –OR groups, and H2O is released.Section 5.3

Molecule in the Clinic: Methyl methacrylate (acrylic resin)

Methyl methacrylate is the monomer of poly(methyl methacrylate), PMMA, the acrylic used for denture bases, custom impression trays and orthopedic bone cement. In the dental lab a liquid monomer is mixed with polymer powder and the C=C double bonds link up by addition polymerization.

Your turn: Draw the repeat unit of PMMA. Which bond in the monomer is used up?

Answer
The C=C double bond opens up: the repeat unit is –CH2–C(CH3)(COOCH3)–. The ester group is not part of the chain; it hangs off every other carbon as a side group.

Molecule in the Clinic: Epinephrine in a dental cartridge

Dental anesthetic cartridges such as “lidocaine with epinephrine 1:100,000” contain epinephrine (adrenaline). It constricts nearby blood vessels, so the anesthetic stays at the site longer and there is less bleeding. Epinephrine is also the hormone and neurotransmitter of the fight-or-flight response.

Your turn: Classify the amine in epinephrine, and name its other functional groups.

Answer
The nitrogen carries two carbons, so it is a secondary amine. The two OH groups on the ring are phenols, and the OH on the side chain is a secondary alcohol.

Your turn: Does epinephrine have a chiral carbon?

Answer
Yes: the side-chain carbon bonded to the OH has four different groups (H, OH, the ring and CH2NHCH3). The body makes and responds to one specific enantiomer.

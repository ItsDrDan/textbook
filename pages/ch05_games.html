How to use this page

These games are free, work on your phone, and keep score in your browser. Play a few rounds after each lecture; a short session every day beats one long one before the exam. Each game opens in a new tab if you prefer a bigger screen.

Mirror Match

Stereochemistry practice: spot chiral carbons, tell enantiomers from diastereomers on wedge-and-dash drawings, cis and trans isomers, and D and L from Fischer projections.

For this chapter: Chiral Carbons and Isomer Relationships (this is the main game for Section 5.5), then Fischer & D/L.

Bond Voyage! Organic Reactions

Identify reaction types, draw products and reactants, and name what you drew.

For this chapter: Alcohol set: reduction and hemiacetal/acetal formation.

Organic Origins

Spot functional groups, classify 1°/2°/3° carbons, name and draw compounds, predict products.

For this chapter: Med Molecules: find the carbonyls in real drugs.

New modes: Rank It: aldehydes and ketones against alkanes, ethers and alcohols of the same molar mass.

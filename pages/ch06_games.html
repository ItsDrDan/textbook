How to use this page

These games are free, work on your phone, and keep score in your browser. Play a few rounds after each lecture; a short session every day beats one long one before the exam. Each game opens in a new tab if you prefer a bigger screen.

Bond Voyage! Organic Reactions

Identify reaction types, draw products and reactants, and name what you drew.

For this chapter: Acid set: esterification, hydrolysis, saponification and neutralization. Try Full Reaction mode.

Organic Origins

Spot functional groups, classify 1°/2°/3° carbons, name and draw compounds, predict products.

For this chapter: Name It for carboxylic acids and esters.

New modes: Rank It (boiling points and solubility of carboxylic acids) and Isomer Sorter (acids and esters as functional group isomers).

Open Organic Origins in a new tab

Proton Patrol

pH and charge practice: decide what each group does at pH 7.4, find the net charge of drugs and amino acids, and name and dissolve salts.

For this chapter: Acids & Bases and Salts: carboxylic acids at pH 7.4, acid strength, carboxylate salts and their solubility (Sections 6.3, 6.4 and 6.10).

How to use this page

These games are free, work on your phone, and keep score in your browser. Play a few rounds after each lecture; a short session every day beats one long one before the exam. Each game opens in a new tab if you prefer a bigger screen.

Organic Origins

Spot functional groups, classify 1°/2°/3° carbons, name and draw compounds, predict products.

For this chapter: Start with Spot the Group, then 1°/2°/3° and Name It (alkanes and cycloalkanes). Draw It checks your condensed and skeletal drawings.

New modes: Isomer Sorter (same compound, constitutional isomers, or not isomers; chain vs position isomers) and Rank It (boiling points by chain length and branching).

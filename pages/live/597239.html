How to use this page

These games are free, work on your phone, and keep score in your browser. Play a few rounds after each lecture; a short session every day beats one long one before the exam. Each game opens in a new tab if you prefer a bigger screen.

Lipid Lab

For this chapter: Fatty Acid ID first, then Build & Break (hydrolysis and saponification), Lipid Line-up and Membranes.

Sticky Molecules

Intermolecular forces in organic and biological molecules: name the forces, win boiling point battles, predict what mixes, and run predict-first labs on hydrogen bonds, cells in salt water and protein folding.

For this chapter: Boiling Point Battles → “Fatty acids: melting points” (Section 9.1), Biomolecules → the membrane and soap questions (Sections 9.2 and 9.5), and Cells & Osmosis for hypotonic, isotonic and hypertonic solutions (Section 9.5).

This page maps every topic in the Fresno City College course outline of record for CHEM 3B (effective Spring 2027, C-ID CHEM 102) to the sections of this book that teach it. Instructors and reviewers can use it to check coverage; students can use it to find the reading for any topic on the syllabus. Each chapter also has a Chapter Guide, Practice Problems and Practice Games that cover the same topics. The chapter-level summary and the student learning outcomes are on the About This Book page.

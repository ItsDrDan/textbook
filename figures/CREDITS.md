## Lecture-slide figures (`Slide*.png`)

The book credits all 41 slide figures as "drawn by the author". The lecture decks were built around Timberlake's *General, Organic, and Biological Chemistry* (Pearson). `slides_text/` contains a Pearson copyright line and a stock-photo credit. So the "drawn by the author" claim can't be accepted without checking.

Check each figure against the original drawing files. Any figure that contains publisher art must be redrawn or removed. A CC license cannot cover it.

| Status | Files | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **UNVERIFIED: likely third-party art** | Slide40, Slide41, Slide43, Slide68, Slide69 | Publisher-style artwork, scanned or recoloured looks, cropped labels. Slide68 is cut off mid-word ("Maximum ra", "Optimum p"). Slide43 contains a stray duplicate fructose and cropped "O"/"OH" glyphs. |
| **UNVERIFIED: pasted elements of unknown origin** | Slide35, Slide38, Slide50, Slide70 | Mix of drawing styles (the Fischer projection and methanol, maltose, cholesterol, ascorbic acid) |
| Probably author-drawn (consistent ChemDraw style) | Slide1, 2, 3, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 19, 21, 22, 23, 24, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 32, 36, 42, 47, 48, 49, 51, 52, 55, 56, 59, 60 | Still needs the author's confirmation |

Errors in the slide figures, seen during the audit. Fix them when the figures are redrawn:
- Slide2: "Normal branched chain" should be "unbranched", and "Isometric" should be "Isomeric".
- Slide15: the Tollens equation is unbalanced.
- Slide28: "three-caron" should be "three-carbon".
- Slide42: an aldehyde is labeled "ketose", and "COH" should be "COOH".
- Slide49: shows 3 H₂O where saponification uses 3 NaOH.

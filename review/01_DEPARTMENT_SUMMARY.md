# CHEM 3B open textbook: summary for department review

**Request:** approve *Introductory Organic and Biological Chemistry for FCC* as the CHEM 3B text [confirm: the COR's wording on OER or substitute texts], and collect faculty reviews with the attached form before it is moved from the LibreTexts Workbench to the Bookshelf.

**Read it:** https://chem.libretexts.org/@go/page/488223 opens without a login. Start with *About This Book* and *For Instructors* in the Front Matter. Two notes for readers: (1) while the book is in the Workbench, the Previous/Next arrows at the top of a page can point to another college's book for signed-out readers, so move between sections with the table of contents; (2) a compiled PDF can be sent instead.

**Author:** Daniel Kwasniewski, Department of Chemistry, Fresno City College. Remixed from three open textbooks (below) and largely rewritten; the companion lab manual is by Alexandra Aloia and Daniel Kwasniewski.

## What it is

- **Scope:** the CHEM 3B course outline of record (effective Spring 2027, C-ID CHEM 102). The outline's seventeen lecture units map onto fifteen chapters; the *Course Outline Alignment* page maps every outline item to the sections that teach it.
- **Chapters:** 1 Bonding review (Lewis structures and intermolecular forces); 2 Alkanes and cycloalkanes; 3 Alkenes, alkynes and aromatics; 4 Alcohols, phenols, ethers and thiols; 5 Aldehydes and ketones; 6 Carboxylic acids and esters; 7 Amines and amides; 8 Carbohydrates; 9 Lipids; 10 Amino acids and proteins; 11 Enzymes and vitamins; 12 Nucleic acids and protein synthesis; 13 Carbohydrate metabolism; 14 Metabolism and energy production; 15 Metabolism of lipids and proteins.
- **Audience:** students headed into nursing, dental hygiene, radiologic technology, respiratory therapy and the other health professions. Every chapter shows where its chemistry appears in a clinic, a pharmacy or a lab result, through clinical boxes and a running patient case (Rosa).
- **Course conventions stated once and used everywhere** (For Instructors lists them): 2.5/1.5 ATP per NADH/FADH₂ and 30–32 ATP per glucose, [H]/[O] for reduction and oxidation, six enzyme classes, acid strength as a ranking (no pKa), no curved-arrow mechanisms, no R/S or E/Z names.

## Accessibility

Built to WCAG 2.1 AA. On November 20 an automated WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA scan (axe-core) of all 199 live pages at phone width (320 px), with every hidden answer opened, found no problems in the book's own content. Every image has a text description, every table has header cells and a caption, every embed has a title, hidden answers use a standard disclosure control, tables and figures that scroll sideways on a phone can be reached from the keyboard, and math is MathJax text that screen readers speak. The PDF is tagged (headings, tables, figure descriptions). One limit comes from LibreTexts, not the book: the glossary pop-ups open by mouse or touch only (the Glossary page has the same definitions). A screen-reader listening test by a person is still to be done.

## Short description (about 50 words)

A free, phone-ready organic and biological chemistry textbook for nursing and allied-health students, written for the CHEM 3B course outline at Fresno City College (C-ID CHEM 102). Every exercise has a worked answer; clinical boxes, a running patient case, auto-graded Try It practice, captioned video lessons, ADAPT homework and a companion lab manual are built in.

## Long description (about 150 words)

*Introductory Organic and Biological Chemistry for FCC* covers the second semester of a general, organic and biological chemistry sequence (C-ID CHEM 102) in 15 chapters: a bonding review, the organic functional groups from alkanes to amides, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, enzymes and vitamins, nucleic acids and protein synthesis, and metabolism. It follows the Fresno City College CHEM 3B course outline and is written for students headed into nursing, dental hygiene, radiologic technology, respiratory therapy and other health careers.

Each section pairs adapted open-textbook reading with a Lecture Connection block of worked examples and Professor Tips, clinical boxes and a running patient case. Valley Connection boxes tie the chemistry to the San Joaquin Valley, with cited sources. All 1,640 exercises and practice problems have worked answers; auto-graded Try It boxes explain every choice, and each chapter has a Chapter Guide and an ADAPT homework assignment. "In the Lab" boxes link a free companion lab manual.
